About Us

Empower women’s voices and participation in all pursuits.

“Oromia Women’s Association” is established for an indefinite period of time in 2001 in accordance with article 404 of 1960 civil code and regulation No.32/1967 proclaimed an association.

To see women of the region in particular, those women members’ of the association equal with men to be participatory in and beneficiary from the ongoing economic, social and political transformation of the country

Enable all women of the region to be directly participatory in and beneficiary from the building of democratic system and good governance by strengthening their awareness level on the importance of association so that they join the association and are empowered in the on going economic, social and political transformation of the country.

Enable women members’ of the association and all women of the region to be directly participatory in and beneficiary from the fruits of the policies and strategies of the country by organizing them after raising their awareness level.
